Taean cable landing station

Korea entry point for subsea cables

The Taean cable landing station is the physical location where international subsea cables join Korea’s network.

  • EAC Korea Backhaul operations
  • Cable landing station facilities and infrastructure operations
  • 24/365 status monitoring
  • Domestic backhaul segment integration
Redundant backhaul

Two routes between Taean and Seoul

Two domestic backhaul routes connect Taean to the Seocho Telehouse: one via Pyeongtaek and Hongseong, and the other via Osan, Cheonan, Daejeon, Iksan, and Daecheon.

  • Taean · Hongseong · Pyeongtaek · Seocho route
  • Taean · Daecheon · Iksan · Daejeon · Cheonan · Osan · Seocho route
  • 24/365 circuit monitoring
  • Customer access segment integration
