Redundant backhaul

Two routes between Taean and Seoul

Two domestic backhaul routes connect the Taean EAC cable landing station to the Seocho Telehouse: a 261 km primary route and a 480 km backup route.

  • Primary route 261 km · Taean · Hongseong · Pyeongtaek · Seocho
  • Backup route 480 km · Taean · Boryeong · Iksan · Daejeon · Cheonan · Osan · Seocho
  • 24/365 circuit monitoring
  • Customer access segment integration
