Circuit configuration

How an international circuit connects

The circuit runs from the domestic customer access segment through the Seoul Telehouse, the Taean cable landing station, the EAC subsea cable, and overseas cable landing stations and POPs to the overseas customer segment.

  1. 01Domestic customerCustomer access segment
  2. 02Seoul TelehouseActivation · interconnection · quality testing
  3. 03Taean cable landing stationDomestic and international circuit connection
  4. 04EAC subsea cableInternational transmission segment
  5. 05Overseas cable landing station · POPOverseas access connection
  6. 06Overseas customerFinal service segment
