History

Key milestones

A summary of key changes in our business and operating model since founding.

Changes in international-circuit operations and foundations
  1. 2001

    Company founded and international-business foundation established

    After incorporation, the company acquired an international NSP business license and entered a domestic backhaul acquisition agreement.

  2. 2002

    Agreement to acquire the Taean EAC cable landing station

    Established the operating foundation for an international subsea cable landing point in Korea.

  3. 2003

    Change in global shareholder

    AGC changed to Asia Netcom.

  4. 2006

    Change in Asia Netcom ownership

    Asia Netcom was acquired by Connect Holdings Ltd.

  5. 2008

    Pacnet Ltd. launched

    The merger of Asia Netcom and Pacific Internet created Pacnet Ltd.

  6. 2010

    Domestic shareholder changed to LG U+

    Following the merger of LG Dacom and LGT, the combined LG Telecom changed its name to LG U+.

  7. 2015

    Global shareholder changed to Telstra

    Following Telstra’s acquisition of Pacnet, the second-largest shareholder changed to Telstra.

  8. Ongoing

    Continued international-circuit operations

    We operate the Taean cable landing station, redundant Taean–Seoul backhaul, the Seocho Telehouse, and a 24/365 monitoring system.
